When you create an account, NO MORE WASTE collects your last name, first name, email address, phone number and postal address, as well as information related to your participation (volunteer applications, collections, distributions, listings, and forum messages).
This data is used only to manage your account, organize food collections and distributions, and connect members of the association. It is never sold to third parties.
Your data is kept for the duration of your membership, then archived or deleted in accordance with applicable regulations.
The site only uses a technical session cookie, required for login and remembering your chosen language. No advertising or tracking cookie is used.
All content on this site (text, logos, layout) is the property of the NO MORE WASTE association unless otherwise stated. Reproduction without permission is prohibited.
NO MORE WASTE is published by the NO MORE WASTE association (French law 1901), whose purpose is to fight food waste by collecting and redistributing food to members and beneficiaries of the association.
Food offered through the platform (collections, distributions, member-to-member listings) is facilitated by the association but remains the responsibility of the respective contributors.
